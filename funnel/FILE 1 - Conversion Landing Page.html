Before you start

What does this cost me?
Nothing. Your first consultation is completely free, with no fees and no catch. It's just a conversation to understand what you're looking for and see how Jacob's side can help.
Am I going to get the hard sell?
No. There's no pushy pitch and no pressure to decide anything. This is a real local conversation about your move, so you set the pace, and you're free to take your time or walk away.
What if I don't have a timeline yet, or I'm just exploring?
Still totally welcome. Plenty of people reach out months (or years) before they make a move. Whether you're ready now or just starting to picture it, it's worth getting a local in your corner early.

It stays private. Your details go straight to Jacob's side to help you, and they're never sold, never spammed, and never handed off to strangers. You'll only hear from people on Jacob's team.
Who will I be talking to?
Someone from Jacob's team. Jacob works with a small group of hand-picked local agents, and you'll be matched with the one who knows your situation and neighborhoods best, so you always get the right local for what you need.
