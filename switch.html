Quick guide

How the Switch works

This page decides which leads come to you and which go to your team. You can change it anytime — new leads follow your changes the moment you Save.

The idea in one line

Each new lead is checked against your rules from the top down, and the first rule it matches decides where it goes. If it matches none of them, the "Send everyone else to…" line at the bottom decides. That's why the order of your rules matters.

How do I…

Send a certain kind of lead to myself
Easiest way: tap a preset chip at the top that's close to what you want, then Save. To do it by hand: + Add rule, tick the answers it applies to under "If a lead matches", set Then send → You, tap Done, then Save changes.
Add a new rule
Tap + Add rule. It opens ready to edit. Under "If a lead matches" tick which answers it applies to (e.g. Looking to: Buy a home), choose You or Team under "Then send", and tap Done. Use the ↑ ↓ arrows to move it to the right spot.
Make a rule more specific (add a second condition)
Open the rule and tap "+ add a condition…", then pick another question (like Timeline or Price range) and tick the answers. When a rule has more than one condition, a lead must match all of them for the rule to apply.
Change where a rule sends leads
Tap the rule to open it, then under "Then send" tap You or Team. Save.
Change the order (and why it matters)
Use the ↑ ↓ arrows on each rule. Because the first matching rule wins, put your most specific / most important rules near the top. If a rule shows a "never runs" note, a rule above it already catches those leads — move it up to fix that.
Use a preset
Tap a chip under "Start from a preset" (e.g. Cash & pre-approved). It fills in a ready-made set of rules you can tweak. Nothing changes until you Save.
Check a change before I save
In the Try it out box, pick a pretend lead's answers. It instantly shows where that lead would be sent — You or Team — with your current rules.
Delete a rule
Tap the trash icon on the rule.
Pause everything when I'm slammed
Flip the overflow switch at the top to ON — every lead goes to your team until you flip it back OFF. While it's on, your rules dim to show they're paused.

What the words mean

Rule
One instruction: "if a lead looks like this, send them to You or Team."
Condition
The "if" part of a rule — an answer the lead gave. A rule can have several; the lead must match all of them.
Looking to
Whether the lead wants to buy, sell, rent short-term (then buy soon), or rent long-term.
Timeline
How soon they want to move — As soon as possible, Sooner than 3 months, 3–6 months, 6–12 months, or 1+ year.
Price range
The budget they picked. (Only buyers and short-term renters are asked this.)
Pre-approval
Whether a buyer is already pre-approved, paying cash, or still needs to be connected with a lender.
You / Team
Where the lead is sent — straight to you, or to a local expert on your team.
Send everyone else to…
The catch-all at the bottom for any lead that matches none of your rules.
Overflow switch
The master toggle up top. ON = every lead goes to the team, no matter the rules.
"Never runs" note
Means a rule above already covers those leads, so this one is never used. Move it higher to fix it.

Saving

Nothing goes live until you press Save changes and type confirm. Changed your mind? Just reload the page to throw away a draft.
